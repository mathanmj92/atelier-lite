# What's new

## 1.0.0

**Atelier Lite: the four office apps, in one workspace.**
- Atelier Lite is Atelier 2.6 with the **Documents, Presentation, Spreadsheet and PDF** apps only. Forms, Markdown, Notes and Whiteboard are not part of it.
- Everything else is the same: the welcome screen with recent workspaces, Home with rename and delete, the tab strip with Home and the workspace next to each other, Settings (General, Appearance, AI assistant, Security, Performance), the AI assistant, workspace locks, links between files (Ctrl+Shift+K), sleeping tabs and Low-power mode, and Report a problem.
- It installs next to the full Atelier without touching it: its own folder, its own settings, and a default workspace folder called `Documents\Atelier Lite`.
